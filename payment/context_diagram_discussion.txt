# Figure 2 Discussion: Context Diagram

Figure 2 illustrates the **Context Diagram** (Data Flow Diagram Level 0) of the **Guidance Counseling Management System (GCMS)**. This diagram defines the system's boundary and its interactions with external entities, highlighting the high-level flow of information.

The **GCMS** acts as the central processing unit, mediating data exchanges between three primary actors: the **Student**, the **Counselor**, and the **Administrator**.

1.  **Student Interaction:** The Student entity initiates the process by providing **Login Credentials** for authentication. Once authenticated, they submit **Appointment Requests** accompanied by **Wellness Intake Data** (responses to physical, intellectual, and environmental wellness questionnaires). In return, the system provides real-time **Appointment Notifications** (confirmations or rescheduling alerts) and updates the **Dashboard Status** to keep the student informed of their request's progress.

2.  **Counselor Interaction:** The Counselor entity interacts with the system to manage their workflow. They input **Session Notes** and specific **Appointment Status Updates** (such as confirming or declining a request). The system reciprocates by providing access to comprehensive **Student Records** and issuing **Schedule Notifications**, ensuring counselors are prepared for upcoming sessions and aware of pending requests.

3.  **Administrator Interaction:** The Administrator entity ensures the system's operational integrity. They provide **User Account Data** to register or update counselor and student profiles and manage **System Configuration** settings. The system outputs analytical **System Reports** and **Activity Logs**, allowing the administrator to monitor system usage and counseling trends effectively.

By isolating these external inputs and outputs, the Context Diagram demonstrates how the GCMS centralizes data management, replacing manual paper-based exchanges with secure, automated digital flows.
